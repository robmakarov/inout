## NEXT — A BLOCKER, NOT A POLISH STEP
THE ANCHOR. `onBatch` dates sample 0 from when batches ARRIVE, and a min-filter strips jitter but
never a constant, so a reader one thread away places the whole take late by the cost of a
postMessage: MEASURED at +14 ms (anchor 88.3 → 102.1 ms) on the first build. The batch now carries
`stampMs = performance.timeOrigin + performance.now()` taken in the worker at flush, and the main
thread converts with its own origin — BUT THE RE-MEASURE CANNOT BE BELIEVED and must be redone:
  1. on a QUIET machine. The re-measure ran at load average 7.5; its main-thread control drifted
     88.3 → 126-136 ms and padded 87 ms on an UNDOSED cell, which has never happened before.
  2. controlling for the rig's own source state — cells come up with the synthetic AudioContext
     either FRESH (`ctx=0.009s`) or long-running (`ctx=15-18s`), and the anchor readings track that
     split. Pin it, or report it per cell.
  3. interleaving the arms, several runs each, exactly as B12's `?audiobuf=0` A/B did.
If the stamp does not hold the anchor where the main-thread reader puts it, the fallbacks are: WARM
the tap worker before the press (the app already warms the encoder at mount for this reason, H6), or
keep the reader on the main thread and let B12's 4 s buffer stand as the answer.

## MEASURED
- PROBE, prod Chrome, 21 s window, 20.0 s of the main thread blocked, processor pinned to the
  platform's own 32-quantum buffer, two reps:
    reader on the main thread   delivered 3.6 / 3.9 s · gap 13.8 / 15.3 s · worst 1730 ms
    reader in the worker        delivered 21.1 / 21.1 s · gap 0.70 / 0.70 s · worst 3 ms
  0.70 s over 21 s is the SOURCE's own floor (33 ms/s, the rate B12's undosed control reads) and
  every step in it is under 3 ms, so the whole 20 s stall costs the worker path nothing.
- THROUGH THE PRODUCT, 45 s takes, buffer cut to 100 ms so only the thread can save it, 32 s blocked:
    reader=main    tapGap 26,739 ms · padded 26,000 ms · worst gap 1637 ms
    reader=worker  tapGap  1,559 ms · padded      0 ms · worst gap    3 ms  (own control 1,528 ms)
- ANCHOR, first build (no stamp), idle: main 88.3 ms → worker 102.1 ms = +14 ms LATE. That is the
  number the stamp exists to remove and the one a clean re-measure has to show as ~0.

## BLOCKED
Nothing external. It needs a quiet machine and the controlled anchor A/B above.

## DO NOT
- Do not land this on the starvation numbers alone. They are excellent and they are not the question:
  a tap that keeps every sample and places it 14 ms late is a sync regression, and sync is Robert's.
- Do not read an anchor off a loaded machine, and never compare two cells whose `ctx=` differ.
- The tap-gap floor is REAL and belongs to the source, not to a defect: ~33 ms/s of sub-6 ms steps on
  this synthetic source, present with the buffer on and off and with the dose at zero. Subtract the
  control before quoting any gap number.
