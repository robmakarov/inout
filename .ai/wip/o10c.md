# O10c — deterministic spectral gating

## DONE (on `task/o10c`, NOT merged — nothing is wired, so main is unaffected)
`src/core/compose/spectralGate.ts` + its tests. Deterministic, no model: STFT (Hann, 1024, 75 %
overlap), a per-bin noise floor from the take's own quiet fifth, a smooth gain, overlap-add back.
Seven tests, all green, and the order is deliberate — the transform's identity is asserted first
because a gate built on a transform that does not reconstruct measures its own error and calls it
noise removal.

## MEASURED
- Reconstruction with nothing gated: **-159 dB** against a -16.7 dB signal. Exact.
- Hann at 75 % overlap sums to **1.5** in the interior — the COLA the overlap-add divides by.
- **ZERO triggers on clean speech and on breathy speech.**
- A steady noise bed under two tone bursts: **2.0 dB** out of the quiet stretch at the conservative
  default with the burst inside 1 dB, and more when `overFloorDb` is raised — so the default is a
  setting and not a ceiling.

## THREE DEFECTS THE TESTS CAUGHT, all of which would have shipped quietly
1. EMPTY BINS. A real signal leaves hundreds of bins at the arithmetic's own dust, and comparing
   small against small trips every time: **36,427 triggers on CLEAN speech**, every one inaudible.
   A bin below silence, or whose FLOOR is below silence, is declined.
2. SIGNAL IS NOT A BED. Level alone cannot tell hiss from a quiet syllable. The gate asks whether a
   bin is STATIONARY (`steadyRatio`: its quiet fifth against its middle — white noise reads ~0.57, a
   bin carrying speech far less). This is what takes speech to zero.
3. A CHANGE TOO SMALL TO COUNT IS A CHANGE TOO SMALL TO MAKE. Bins were nudged under 0.1 dB without
   being counted, so "zero triggers" still came back 20 dB from the input.
Plus the edge guard: dividing by a window sum of 0.001 AMPLIFIES, which read as the gate making a
noise bed **4.3 dB LOUDER** when measured over the whole array.

## THE STREAMING SEAM IS BUILT AND PROVEN (2026-09-04)
`StreamingGate` carries state across chunk boundaries and is SAMPLE-IDENTICAL to gating the whole
signal — not similar, identical: the interior differs by more than 100 dB below the signal and the
TRIGGER COUNTS MATCH EXACTLY, so it gated the same things and not merely a similar amount. A ragged
feed (1000, 7777, 129, 30000 samples) matches an even one to the same bound, so the chunk size is not
a parameter.
IT TOOK ONE REWRITE AND THE REASON IS WORTH KEEPING: the first cut mixed a buffer-relative frame
start with a count of samples already emitted. That agrees with itself on an even feed and DRIFTS on
a ragged one — the streamed output came back 24 dB from the whole-signal output. Everything is an
index into one absolute timeline now, which cannot drift.
