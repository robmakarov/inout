# O10c — deterministic spectral gating

## DONE (on `task/o10c`, NOT merged — nothing is wired, so main is unaffected)
`src/core/compose/spectralGate.ts` + its tests. Deterministic, no model: STFT (Hann, 1024, 75 %
overlap), a per-bin noise floor from the take's own quiet fifth, a smooth gain, overlap-add back.
Seven tests, all green, and the order is deliberate — the transform's identity is asserted first
because a gate built on a transform that does not reconstruct measures its own error and calls it
noise removal.

## MEASURED
- Reconstruction with nothing gated: **-159 dB** against a -16.7 dB signal. Exact.
- Hann at 75 % overlap sums to **1.5** in the interior — the COLA the overlap-add divides by.
- **ZERO triggers on clean speech and on breathy speech.**
- A steady noise bed under two tone bursts: **2.0 dB** out of the quiet stretch at the conservative
  default with the burst inside 1 dB, and more when `overFloorDb` is raised — so the default is a
  setting and not a ceiling.

## THREE DEFECTS THE TESTS CAUGHT, all of which would have shipped quietly
1. EMPTY BINS. A real signal leaves hundreds of bins at the arithmetic's own dust, and comparing
   small against small trips every time: **36,427 triggers on CLEAN speech**, every one inaudible.
   A bin below silence, or whose FLOOR is below silence, is declined.
2. SIGNAL IS NOT A BED. Level alone cannot tell hiss from a quiet syllable. The gate asks whether a
   bin is STATIONARY (`steadyRatio`: its quiet fifth against its middle — white noise reads ~0.57, a
   bin carrying speech far less). This is what takes speech to zero.
3. A CHANGE TOO SMALL TO COUNT IS A CHANGE TOO SMALL TO MAKE. Bins were nudged under 0.1 dB without
   being counted, so "zero triggers" still came back 20 dB from the input.
Plus the edge guard: dividing by a window sum of 0.001 AMPLIFIES, which read as the gate making a
noise bed **4.3 dB LOUDER** when measured over the whole array.

## NEXT — THE WIRING, AND THE ONE HARD PART IS NAMED
1. THE STREAMING SEAM, which is the only real design left. `render.ts` writes audio one second at a
   time (`writeAudioChunk`), and the gate needs frames that straddle those boundaries: a chunk gated
   on its own would ramp in and out at every second. What it needs is a carry — keep the last
   `GATE_FRAME - GATE_HOP` samples and the partial overlap-add tail, and emit only the samples whose
   window sum is complete. It is ~40 lines and it is TESTABLE WITHOUT A BROWSER: gate a signal whole,
   gate it in one-second pieces through the carry, and assert the two are the same to -100 dB. Write
   that test FIRST — a seam that clicks once a second is exactly the defect B13 is about.
2. THE PROFILE'S SOURCE. It needs the take's quiet frames before gating starts, and the render is a
   forward stream. Do NOT decode twice: X1's capture envelope and B1b's `chooseWindow` already pick
   representative seconds off the packet index without decoding, and the profile should be built from
   a bounded probe the same way (budget it, and fall back to the first N seconds).
3. Flag + panel row + docs/FLAGS.md, modelled exactly on `audioTracks.ts` (URL → override → storage →
   default, and the worker is TOLD via pipeline.ts's flags block; a worker has no localStorage).
   IT MUST GO INTO `RenderFlagPrint` (chunkPlan.ts) TOO — O10b's own switch was forgotten there and
   the first press was served a stale pre-render.
4. Gates: OFF byte-identical (pinned) · zero triggers on clean speech through the PRODUCT, not only
   the unit fixture · fidelity green · oracle green · A/B files to ~/Downloads for Robert's ear
   before any default moves.

## DO NOT
- Do not move the default. It is OFF, and a default that a user can hear is Robert's alone.
- Do not tune `steadyRatio` against the synthetic speech fixture in this file until the product path
  exists — B13 has already produced three false answers from instruments measuring their own
  material, and this file's fixtures are its own material too.
