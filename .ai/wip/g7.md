## THE DESIGN DECISION THAT COSTS A SESSION IF IT IS RE-DERIVED
A TAKE RUNS WITH THIS DOCUMENT HIDDEN (Robert records his screen and switches away),
and a hidden Chrome tab clamps setInterval to ~1 Hz — E1 measured it: a 16 ms main-thread
ticker read 984 ms late AT IDLE and 3568 ms under a real 3 s burn (core/pressure.ts header).
So a main-thread timer CANNOT be the clock for capture-time lateness: it would report the
clamp as a stall on every take. The clock has to live on a thread that is not throttled
(E1: a worker ticker in the same hidden page ran 59 Hz, max 27 ms late), and the main thread
only STAMPS ARRIVALS. That is the instrument: worker beats, main-thread arrival, lateness =
arrival − due, both converted through performance.timeOrigin.
